# Decision Log: [Project code]

> **Guide.** Log (METHOD.md section 6): added to, never edited. One entry per decision the PO settles that changes a current document. The decision's content lives in the document it changed; the entry says when, who, what in one line, why in one line, and where. A decision inside one build plan stays in that plan (`D1`, `P1`) and isn't logged here unless it changes a current document.
>
> IDs are `DEC-NNN`, three digits, never reused. A project carried over from an earlier log keeps its numbers.
>
> Correct: `DEC-127 · 2026-11-14 · PO · Site and terms removed from school structure · No school in the target segment runs separate sites · requirements/requirements.md#school-structure`
> Incorrect: a paragraph retelling the discussion, or an entry with no link to the document it changed.
