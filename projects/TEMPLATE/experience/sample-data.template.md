# Sample Data: [Project code]

> **Guide.** Living (METHOD.md section 6). The one realistic, consistent set of sample data every artboard uses, and every unit's seed reproduces (METHOD.md section 7.3). The same customers, people, records and moment across all screens, so a figure on one screen agrees with every other, and a built page can be compared with its artboard.
>
> - **Realistic in size and content:** more rows than the smallest page size; names, places, amounts and references as real customers would have them.
> - **Consistent:** a value appears with the same spelling and figure everywhere. A design group that adds a record adds it here first.
> - **Seeded, not invented:** a unit's seed takes its records from here and names them in its plan (A10). A record a unit needs that isn't here is added here first.
> - **Fictional:** never a real customer's or person's data.
